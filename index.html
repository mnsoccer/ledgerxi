Import from CSV

Bring a past worksheet in. Works best for single-event spreadsheets with a description column plus rate/qty columns for budget and actual. You'll set row types (revenue, expense, group, skip) and confirm column mappings below. Multi-event sheets (e.g. multiple leagues in one file) should be split before import.
Step 01Choose a file
Drop a .csv file here, or click to browse
Tip: if your spreadsheet is in Excel or Google Sheets, export as CSV first
📋 Paste from Google Sheets
Select your cells in Google Sheets, copy (Ctrl/Cmd+C), and paste below.
🔗 Google Sheet link
Sheet must be shared as "Anyone with the link can view".
Step 02Event details
Auto-filled from filename and defaults
Partner split will be copied from your Settings default. Adjust per-event after importing.
Step 03Map columns and rows
Click any dropdown to reassign. Group rows inherit their label from the description cell; revenue rows below a group row belong to that group.
